01 / About

Hands-on developer, happiest where code meets the real world.

For the past three years I worked at SpectralBot as a C# / .NET developer. My main focus was our Unity application: software that finds AV devices on the network, talks to them and lets people control them from one place. That meant a lot more than writing UI. I dealt with networking, device communication, debugging on real hardware and making things reliable on site.

I came into this through a bachelor's degree in XR development, where I worked on projects together with companies like Siemens, CNH Industrial and SCOREPION. That's where I learned Unity, C#, AR on mobile and VR on headsets.

SpectralBot recently closed its doors, so I'm looking for what's next. I want work that's hands-on and close to real systems: AV technology, broader software development, or the data & AI side, where I'd like to grow.
